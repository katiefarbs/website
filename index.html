Questions

I barely have time. Will this work for me?

Yes. That’s exactly who it’s for. We start with what you can actually do this week, even if that’s two short workouts.

I’ve tried before and always quit.

Most women I work with have. This time you’re not doing it alone. When you miss a day, we adjust the plan instead of starting over.

How is this different from a free program online?

A free program gives you workouts and leaves the rest to you. With me, you get a plan built around your week, a call every week, and someone who notices when you start slipping and pulls you back in. That’s the part you can’t download.

Is online coaching as good as in person?

For staying consistent, it’s often better. I’m with you all week, not just for one hour in a gym, so I’m there on the days you’d usually quit.

Do I need a gym?

No. Your plan is built around what you have, whether that’s a full gym, a home setup or a hotel room.

What happens on the call, and what does it cost?

We talk about your goals, what’s gotten in the way before, and whether we’re the right fit. If we are, I’ll walk you through the program and the investment. If we’re not, I’ll tell you honestly and point you in the right direction.
