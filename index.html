Questions

I barely have time. Will this work for me?

Yes. That’s exactly who it’s for. Your plan fits around your schedule, not the other way around, and we start with what you can actually do this week.

I’ve tried before and always quit.

Most women I talk to have. The plan was never the problem. Staying consistent is, and that’s what we work on together every single week.

Is online coaching as good as in person?

For staying consistent, it’s often better. You get a plan for your real week, a call with me every week, and you can message me between calls. I’m with you on the days you’d usually quit, not just for one hour in a gym.

Do I need a gym?

No. Your plan is built around what you have, whether that’s a full gym, a home setup or a hotel room.

Is this a diet?

No. You’ll build simple, flexible habits around food, with no strict rules. Coaching isn’t treatment for eating disorders, so if that’s something you’re going through, please speak with a medical professional first.

What happens on the call, and what does it cost?

We talk about your goals, what’s gotten in the way before, and whether coaching with me is the right fit. If it is, I’ll walk you through how it works and the investment. If it isn’t, I’ll tell you honestly.
