Privacy

Last updated November 2026. This page explains what information Katie Farbs Fitness (run by Kaitlyn Farberman) collects through this website and how it’s used.

What I collect

When you book a call, the booking tool collects your name, email, time zone and the answers you give to the booking questions. If you message me on Instagram or email me, I see what you send.

How I use it

Only to run your call, follow up with you about coaching, and, if you become a client, to coach you. I don’t sell your information.

Tools this site uses

Bookings are handled by a scheduling service, and payments by Stripe. This site may use the Meta Pixel to measure how people find it through Instagram and Facebook ads. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time. Email katiefarbsfitness@gmail.com.
